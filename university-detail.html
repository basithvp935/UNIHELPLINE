Admissions

Eligibility & Entry Requirements

Standard criteria for international applicants. Our counsellors offer complete assessment.

Undergraduate (UG) Criteria

Minimum 60% – 70% in Higher Secondary (12th Standard) with relevant subject prerequisites.

Postgraduate (PG / Master's)

Recognised Bachelor's degree with 55% – 65% aggregate score. Work experience required for certain MBAs.

English Proficiency

IELTS 6.0 – 6.5 (min 5.5 in each band), PTE 58 – 65, Duolingo 105 – 120, or Medium of Instruction (MOI) waiver.

Document Checklist

Passports, Academic Transcripts, Statement of Purpose (SOP), 2 Letters of Recommendation (LOR) & CV.

International Scholarships Available

Merit-based bursaries and fee waivers ranging from £1,500 to £5,000+ for eligible applicants.
